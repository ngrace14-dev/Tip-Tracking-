Terminology & Definitions Key

Master Distributor / Marketplace (e.g., Nabis)
A massive third-party logistics (3PL) and fulfillment hub that centralizes brand distribution. Instead of retailers paying dozens of individual brands directly, they order through the platform and issue a single bulk payment to the Master Distributor (e.g., Payee: Nabifive), who then routes the funds to the respective brands.
Ancillary / Merchandise
A non-plant-touching vendor that provides operational supplies, merchandise, software, or services (e.g., packaging, t-shirts, consulting) rather than wholesale cannabis products.
DBA (Doing Business As)
The operational trade name or consumer-facing brand name a company uses, which differs from its legal, registered corporate name on the AP invoice.
Consolidate
Indicates that multiple AP (Accounts Payable) entries exist for the same parent company and should be merged in the accounting system to prevent duplicate payments and confusion.
Defunct / Expired / Revoked / Receivership
The vendor's state cannabis license is no longer active, or the business has folded. Unless there is outstanding legacy debt to pay off, these accounts can be safely archived.
Microbusiness
A specific California commercial license type that allows a single corporate entity to legally cultivate, manufacture, and distribute their own products directly to retail, meaning they act as their own distributor.
3PL / Carrier / Transport
A third-party logistics company that securely transports or stores cannabis goods for other brands but does not own, manufacture, or sell the products themselves.
Non-Cannabis
Vendors providing standard regional business services (e.g., HVAC repair, property management, traditional agriculture, goat grazing) completely unrelated to the cannabis supply chain.
